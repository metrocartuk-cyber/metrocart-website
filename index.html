Frequently asked questions

Trade ordering, made clearer

Who can open a Metrocart trade account?

We work with retailers, wholesalers, food-service operators, and other eligible business buyers. Contact our trade desk with your business details to discuss suitability.

Do you have minimum order quantities?

Minimum quantities vary by product and delivery route. Carton information is shown in the catalogue, and the team will confirm the full requirement before you order.

Where do you deliver?

We coordinate delivery across the United Kingdom, subject to route availability, order size, and product requirements.

How do I request current pricing?

Use the trade enquiry form or contact the operations team directly. Pricing is confirmed against the product, volume, and delivery requirement.

Can you source products not shown in the catalogue?

Yes. Share the brand, format, volume, and target timing with us, and we will review available sourcing options.

Do you provide consultancy and legal support?

Metrocart also provides business consultancy and coordinated legal-service support. Visit the relevant service section to submit a focused enquiry.
